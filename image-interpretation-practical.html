Techniques in Image Interpretation & Remote Sensing - Practical Reflection

In this practical task, I learned key image interpretation and remote sensing techniques. We learned how drone imagery works and performed Land Use Land Cover (LULC) classification, which gave a detailed view of surface features.

I also downloaded satellite data from different geospatial portals and created various color composite maps to highlight specific land cover types. Georeferencing drone images was challenging due to spatial alignment issues, but overcoming those errors significantly improved my practical workflow.

Overall, this hands-on exercise enhanced my confidence in drone data handling and image interpretation.
